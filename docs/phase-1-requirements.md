# Phase-1 Requirements: Amman Earth Movers Quotation Generator

## Business Goal
To streamline and automate the process of generating professional, consistently branded quotations for Amman Earth Movers. The application aims to reduce manual data entry errors, save time for sales and management teams, and provide clients with a clear, trustworthy, and branded quotation document that reflects the company's professional image.

## Core Requirements (Phase 1)
- Provide a clean, intuitive web-based interface for data entry (client details, equipment selection, rates, and duration).
- Generate a live preview of the quotation before finalizing.
- Output a professional document (e.g., printer-friendly view or PDF) that can be sent to the client.
- Ensure all visual outputs strictly follow the Amman Earth Movers branding guidelines based on the provided logo.

## Core Modules (Future Implementation)
1. **Client Management Module:** Form sections to input and temporarily manage customer details (name, company, contact info).
2. **Inventory/Equipment Module:** Selection interface for earth-moving equipment, operators, and standard base rates.
3. **Calculation Engine:** Logic to accurately compute subtotals, taxes, discounts, and final amounts based on hourly or daily rates.
4. **Document Generation Module:** A specialized view to render the final quotation template into a highly formatted, exportable layout.
5. **Dashboard/Layout Wrapper:** High-level structure to navigate between quotation creation and any future settings.
